## Student Mobility for Studies

The **Student Mobility for Studies** action enables students at higher education institutions to spend an integrated period of study of **3 to 12 months** in another participating European country.

### Objectives
- To enable students to benefit educationally, linguistically, and culturally from studying in other European countries  
- To promote cooperation between institutions and enrich the educational environment of host institutions  
- To contribute to the development of well-qualified, open-minded, and internationally experienced future professionals  

### Who Can Benefit?
- Students registered at a higher education institution holding an **Erasmus University Charter**

### Duration
- 3 to 12 months  
- At least one full academic trimester/term  

### Arrangements for Erasmus Students
The study period must be covered by a **grant agreement** between the student and the home institution.

#### Before Departure
Students receive:
- A **Learning Agreement** approved by the student, home institution, and host institution  
- An **Erasmus Student Charter** outlining rights and obligations  

#### After the Study Period
- The host institution provides a **Transcript of Records**  
- The home institution grants **full academic recognition**, preferably using **ECTS credits**

### Financial Support
- Erasmus grants may cover travel and subsistence costs (including insurance)  
- Erasmus students are **exempt from tuition, registration, examination, and library/lab fees**  
- National grants or loans should continue during the Erasmus period  
- Erasmus Mundus students may also receive grants  

### Language Courses
- Erasmus Intensive Language Courses (EILC) may be available before the study period  

### Eligibility & Application
- Institutions must have an **inter-institutional agreement**
- Student must:
  - Be a citizen/resident/refugee of a participating country  
  - Be enrolled in at least the **second year** of higher education  
  - Be studying toward a recognized degree (up to doctorate level)  
- Applications are made through the **home institution’s Erasmus Office**

**Source:** European Commission – Erasmus Studies

## Student Mobility for Placements (Internships)

The **Erasmus Student Mobility for Placements** enables students to complete a traineeship/internship abroad for **3 to 12 months**.

### Objectives
- Adapt students to the EU labor market  
- Develop professional, linguistic, and intercultural skills  
- Promote cooperation between universities and enterprises  

### Who Can Benefit?
- Students from institutions holding an **Extended Erasmus University Charter**

### Duration
- 3 to 12 months  
- Minimum of two weeks for short-cycle vocational education  

### Host Organizations
- Enterprises, training centers, research centers, or higher education institutions  
- Excludes EU institutions, EU programme organizations, and embassies  

### Arrangements
- Covered by a **Placement (Training) Agreement**

#### Before Departure
Students receive:
- A **Training Agreement**
- A **Quality Commitment**
- The **Erasmus Student Charter**

#### After Completion
- Full academic recognition by the home institution  
- If not part of the curriculum, recognition via **Diploma Supplement**

### Financial Support
- Erasmus grants may cover travel and living expenses  
- Additional financial or in-kind support may be provided by host organizations  

### Language Courses
- Erasmus Intensive Language Courses may be available before placement  

### Eligibility & Application
- Student must be registered at an institution with an Extended Erasmus Charter  
- At least one of the sending or receiving countries must be an EU Member State  
- Applications are handled through the home institution’s Erasmus Office  

**Source:** European Commission – Erasmus Placements

## Erasmus Intensive Language Courses (EILC)

EILC are specialized language courses in less widely taught European languages.

### Key Features
- Duration: **2–6 weeks**
- Minimum: **60 total teaching hours**
- At least **15 hours per week**

### Financial Support
- Supplementary mobility grants may be awarded  
- No tuition fee (optional excursion fees may apply)

### Participating Countries
Belgium (Flemish Community), Bulgaria, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Malta, Netherlands, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Sweden, Turkey

### Eligibility
- Erasmus study or placement students  
- Students whose main field is the host language are normally **not eligible**  
- Comenius Assistants may participate if places are available  

### Application
- Applications are submitted through the **home institution**
- Selection is coordinated by the **National Agency** and host institution  

**Source:** European Commission – EILC
