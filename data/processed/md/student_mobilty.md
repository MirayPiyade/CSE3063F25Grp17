## Student Mobility for Studies

The **Student Mobility for Studies** action enables students at higher education institutions to spend an integrated period of study between **3 and 12 months** in another participating European country.

### Objectives
- Enable students to benefit educationally, linguistically, and culturally from learning in other European countries
- Promote cooperation between institutions and enrich the educational environment of host institutions
- Contribute to the development of well-qualified, open-minded, and internationally experienced future professionals

### Who Can Benefit?
- Students registered at a higher education institution holding an **ERASMUS University Charter**

### Duration
- **3 to 12 months**
- At least a full academic trimester/term

### Arrangements for Erasmus Students
- The mobility period must be covered by a **grant agreement** between the student and the home institution

#### Prior to Departure
- **Learning Agreement** approved by the student, home institution, and host institution  
- **Erasmus Student Charter** outlining rights and obligations  
- Example: Learning Agreement from the University of Valladolid

#### At the End of the Period
- Host institution provides a **Transcript of Records**
- Home institution grants **full academic recognition**, preferably using **ECTS credits**

### Financial Support and Fees
- Erasmus grants may cover travel and subsistence costs (including insurance)
- Erasmus students are **exempt from tuition, registration, examination, laboratory, and library fees**
- National grants or loans continue during the Erasmus period
- Erasmus Mundus Master students may also receive Erasmus grants

### Language Courses
- Optional **Erasmus Intensive Language Courses (EILC)** may be attended before mobility
- Additional grants may be awarded

### Eligibility Criteria
- Home and host institutions must hold an Erasmus University Charter
- Student must:
  - Be a citizen of a participating country or have refugee/stateless/permanent resident status
  - Be enrolled in higher education (minimum second year)
  - Be studying toward a recognised degree (up to doctorate level)
- At least one of the countries must be an **EU Member State**

### How to Apply
- Contact the **International / Erasmus Office** of the home institution

**Source:** European Commission  
http://ec.europa.eu/education/erasmus/doc892_en.htm

## Student Mobility for Placements (Traineeships)

The **Erasmus Student Mobility for Placements** enables students to undertake a traineeship/internship abroad between **3 and 12 months**.

### Objectives
- Help students adapt to the EU labour market
- Develop professional, linguistic, and intercultural skills
- Promote cooperation between higher education institutions and enterprises
- Develop internationally experienced future professionals

### Who Can Benefit?
- Students registered at institutions holding an **Extended Erasmus University Charter**

### Duration
- **3–12 months**
- Minimum **2 weeks** for short-cycle higher vocational education

### Where Can Placements Take Place?
- Enterprises, training centres, research centres, and higher education institutions
- In one of **31 participating European countries**
- **Excluded:**
  - EU institutions
  - Organisations managing EU programmes
  - National diplomatic representations

### Arrangements
- Covered by a **Placement / Grant Agreement**

#### Prior to Departure
- **Training Agreement** approved by home institution and host organisation
- **Quality Commitment**
- **Erasmus Student Charter**

#### At the End of the Period
- Full academic recognition using **ECTS**, where possible
- If not part of curriculum, recognition via **Diploma Supplement**

### Financial Support
- Erasmus grant for travel and subsistence
- Possible financial or in-kind contribution from host organisation

### Language Courses
- Optional **Erasmus Intensive Language Courses (EILC)** before placement

### Eligibility
- Student must be registered at an institution with an Extended Erasmus University Charter
- Citizenship or recognised legal status required
- At least one country must be an EU Member State

### How to Apply
- Apply through the **International / Erasmus Office** of the home institution

**Source:** European Commission  
http://ec.europa.eu/education/erasmus/doc894_en.htm

## Erasmus Intensive Language Courses (EILC)

EILC are specialised courses in **less widely used and less taught languages**, designed to prepare students for Erasmus study or placement periods.

### Course Details
- Duration: **2–6 weeks**
- Minimum **60 teaching hours**
- At least **15 hours per week**

### Financial Support and Fees
- Supplementary mobility grants may be provided
- No tuition fees for EILC
- Fees may apply for excursions or optional events

### Countries Offering EILC
Belgium (Flemish Community), Bulgaria, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Malta, Netherlands, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Sweden, Turkey

### Who Can Benefit?
- Erasmus students selected for study or placement in EILC countries
- Students whose major is the host language are generally **not eligible**
- Comenius Assistants may participate if places are available

### Application Process
- Students apply through their **home institution**
- Selection is coordinated by:
  - EILC organising institution
  - National Agency in the host country

**Source:**  
http://ec.europa.eu/education/erasmus/doc902_en.htm
