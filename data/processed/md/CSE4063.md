## Objective & Content
The course provides foundational knowledge of data mining tools and techniques.
**Content:** Data preprocessing, Data warehousing, OLAP, frequent pattern mining (association/correlation), Classification (Trees, Bayes, SVM, ANN), and Clustering (Hierarchical, Density-based).
