# Administrative Functions of Coordinators

- **Exchange Program Coordinator** is assigned by the Dean's Office to conduct Erasmus-related procedures on behalf of the **Faculty**.
- **Department Exchange Coordinator** is assigned by the Dean's Office to conduct Erasmus-related procedures on behalf of the **Department**.
- **Institute Exchange Coordinator** is assigned by the Dean's Office to conduct Erasmus-related procedures on behalf of the **Institute**.
